eClean Privacy Policy

AI-Powered Civic Work Verification Platform

Last updated: April 4, 2026

eClean is an AI-powered platform that connects civic sanitation workers with buyers who need public areas cleaned. Workers upload before-and-after photos, our AI verifies the work, and payment is released automatically. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name and email address — required to create your account
  • Phone number — optional, used for account recovery and notifications
  • Role — whether you are a Worker, Buyer, Citizen, or Supervisor

Location Data

  • GPS coordinates during active tasks — collected every 30 seconds while a task is in progress, including when the app is in the background
  • Location when posting a task — so workers can find nearby jobs

Camera and Photos

  • Before, after, and proof photos — captured through the app's camera during tasks
  • Photos are uploaded and stored on Cloudinary (a cloud storage service)

Device Sensors

  • Accelerometer — to verify physical activity (e.g., distinguishing cleaning motion from inactivity)
  • Magnetometer, barometer, and ambient light sensor — used to improve verification accuracy

Payment Information

  • Payments are processed by Razorpay — we do not store your card numbers, bank account details, or UPI PINs
  • We store only transaction IDs and payment status for record-keeping

Device Information

  • Device model, OS version, and device ID — used for fraud prevention and troubleshooting

Push Notification Tokens

  • We store your device's push notification token to send you task updates, payment confirmations, and important alerts

2. How We Use Your Information

  • AI-powered photo analysis — Our AI (powered by OpenAI GPT-5) examines your before-and-after photos to verify that cleaning work was actually completed
  • GPS trail verification — We use your location history during a task to confirm that you were physically present at the work site
  • Motion verification — Sensor data helps confirm physical cleaning activity took place
  • Dispute resolution — All verification data (photos, GPS, sensor readings) is retained so that any disputes between workers and buyers can be reviewed fairly
  • Payments — To process and track payments between buyers and workers
  • App improvements — Aggregated, anonymized data helps us improve the platform

3. How Long We Keep Your Data

Data Type Retention Period
Task records (details, status, payments) 2 years — required for dispute resolution and legal compliance
GPS location logs 90 days, then permanently deleted
Photos (before, after, proof) Retained with the task record (up to 2 years)
Account information Until you request deletion of your account
Sensor data 90 days, then permanently deleted

4. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Cloudinary — for photo storage
  • OpenAI — photos are sent for AI verification analysis
  • Razorpay — for payment processing
  • Law enforcement or regulators — only when required by law or a valid court order

5. How We Protect Your Data

  • Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted device storage (not in plain text)
  • All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS encryption
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — we never store plain-text passwords
  • Payment data is handled entirely by Razorpay, which is PCI-DSS compliant
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel only

6. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Ask us to correct any inaccurate information
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent — You can stop location tracking by ending your active task or revoking location permissions in your device settings
  • Grievance redressal — Contact us with any concerns, and we will respond within 30 days

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below. We will verify your identity and respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

eClean is not intended for use by anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
