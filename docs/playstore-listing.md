## 3. Full Description

eClean is India's first AI-powered civic work verification platform that connects municipal buyers with sanitation workers. No more paperwork. No more disputes. AI verifies the work, payment releases automatically.

**How It Works**

1. Buyer posts a cleaning task (street, park, drain, public toilet) with location, photos, and budget.
2. Nearby workers see the task on a live map and accept it.
3. Worker arrives on-site, takes BEFORE photos, and starts cleaning.
4. Worker takes AFTER photos when done and submits for verification.
5. Claude AI analyses the before/after photos and scores the cleaning quality.
6. If verified, payment releases instantly to the worker's wallet via Razorpay.

No middlemen. No delays. Fair pay for honest work.

**Key Features**

- AI Photo Verification — Advanced AI compares before and after photos to confirm work was actually done. No human bias, no favouritism.
- GPS Live Tracking — Buyers can track workers in real-time on a live map once work begins. Full transparency.
- Secure Payments — All payments processed through Razorpay. Escrow system holds funds until AI verification passes. Workers get paid the same day.
- Real-Time Chat — Buyers and workers can communicate directly within each task. Share instructions, updates, and photos.
- Offline Support — Poor network? No problem. Photos and data sync automatically when connectivity returns.
- Smart Task Matching — Workers see nearby open tasks sorted by distance, urgency, and payout. Pick the work that suits you.

**For Workers — Earn Money Cleaning Your City**

- Browse and accept cleaning tasks near you
- No interview, no fixed hours — work when you want
- Fair AI-based verification — no rejection without reason
- Instant payment to your wallet after verification
- Track your earnings, ratings, and work history
- Build a reputation with consistent quality scores

**For Buyers — Get Verified Cleaning Results**

- Post tasks in minutes with location pin and photos
- Set your budget and urgency level
- Track worker arrival and progress on a live map
- AI-verified before/after proof — no need to visit the site
- Rate workers and build a trusted team
- Full task history with photo evidence

**For Supervisors**

- Monitor zone cleanliness across your assigned areas
- Review AI verification scores and flag disputes
- Track worker performance and attendance

**For Citizens**

- Report dirty areas in your neighbourhood
- Upload photos of civic issues
- Track cleanup status after reporting

**Available In**

Currently serving Hyderabad, Telangana. Expanding to more cities soon.

**Who Is This For?**

- Municipal corporations and urban local bodies
- Facility management companies
- Housing societies and commercial complexes
- Independent sanitation workers looking for gig work
- Citizens who want cleaner public spaces

**Built in India, for India.**

eClean is designed for real-world conditions — low-bandwidth networks, budget smartphones, and multilingual users. The app is lightweight, fast, and works reliably even on 2G/3G connections.

Download eClean today. Clean your city. Earn fair pay.
